Three steps to the wanted board

A browser and one photograph is the whole setup.

1

Your selfie

One clear photograph of your face, looking at the camera. Good light helps; a hat and sunglasses do not.

2

The Sunfall relight

Your portrait is relit for the Sunfall coast at golden hour. Your face is not redrawn, only the light changes.

3

The bulletin

A numbered bulletin, a permanent link to it, and two short videos to post.

The bounty

Every bulletin carries a reward. The office sets it the moment you are booked, prints it under your charges, and it never changes. Nobody gets to pick their own number.

What the office issues

One payment, three files, yours to keep.

The bulletin file

Your numbered card at full resolution with no watermark, plus a link that stays live so you can send it to anyone.

The bulletin videos

A ten second clip of you on the Sunfall strip at dusk, and an eight second clip of the bulletin turning in the light. Both vertical, both ready to post.

They are made after payment and download themselves when they are done.
